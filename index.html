The evaluation framework

Different assets.
The same standard of evidence.

Property evaluation

Examine comparable sales, property condition, taxes, operating costs, and rental assumptions. Consider both the buyer’s position and the investor’s return.

Needs: verified property identity, dated comparables, and explicit cost assumptions.

Land evaluation

Consider permitted use, access, utilities, environmental constraints, and development costs. Distinguish what exists today from what depends on approvals or future infrastructure.

Needs: parcel records, local authority sources, and site-specific constraints.

Business & market evaluation

Explore customer demand, competitive positioning, revenue quality, margins, and market conditions. Separate an attractive story from a supported financial opportunity.

Needs: sourced market evidence, business records, and transparent assumptions.

Digital currency evaluation

Consider liquidity, price behavior, asset concentration, and source quality. Examine potential patterns alongside alternative explanations and downside scenarios.

Needs: timestamped market data and clearly identified evidence gaps.

These areas describe the intended CERTIS evaluation scope. This page does not produce a live valuation, appraisal, or market assessment.

Precision is the foundation

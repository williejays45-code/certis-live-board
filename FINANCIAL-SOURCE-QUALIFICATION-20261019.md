## Implemented boundaries

`backend/financial-source-transport.mjs` permits only three fixed source templates: Treasury Notes average interest rates, BLS CUUR0000SA0, and SEC submissions for an exact ten-digit CIK. Public API callers cannot choose a URL or outbound headers. DNS IPv4 addresses are checked inside the TLS connection lookup; all returned addresses must pass the public-address filter. IPv6 is deliberately unsupported. TLS certificate verification and the publisher hostname remain enabled. Requests use a dedicated connection, ignore inherited proxy settings, reject redirects, accept uncompressed JSON only, enforce a two-MiB body and ten-second overall deadline, and never retry. There is no polling or production registration. SEC additionally requires an operator-declared contact User-Agent. This checkout's transport has been exercised against Treasury and BLS; SEC live access remains untested.

`backend/financial-sources.mjs` hashes original bytes before normalization, binds subject/units/periods and rejects future dates, duplicates and publisher errors. Treasury percent values retain three decimal places as `percent_milli`. They are **average interest rates on outstanding Treasury Notes**, not par yields, executable prices, or inputs to the existing 2-year/10-year par-yield calculation. BLS index values retain three decimals as `index_milli_1982_84_100`; months retain start/end dates. Annual-average M13 data is rejected. A publisher `-` is a missing observation, never zero. SEC retains accession/form/amendment and filing date; a filing's presence does not authenticate issuer accounting or supply a market price. Publication timestamps are unknown where not provided; retrieval does not stand in for publication. Publisher groups do not establish independent corroboration.

The source contract intentionally differs from the earlier structural-admission schema, which has no index-scale or monthly-period types. No silent coercion or automatic admission into protected runtime evidence is implemented. New normalized IDs conform to the existing financial reference-ID contract. Raw bytes must be retained in an approved evidence store before production use; this package provides no durable store. `compareSourceRevisions` reparses two retained byte payloads and compares a declared overlap window, distinguishing changed bytes from changed observations. Missing current rows are labeled not observed rather than deleted. A failed acquisition returns no candidate or successful-refresh timestamp.

`backend/financial-analysis-contracts.mjs` defines missing/invalid-data contracts for stock, ETF, bond, macro and business inputs. These are model prerequisites, **not new valuation or risk models**. Bond inputs require coupon, modified duration, maturity, credit and liquidity references. ETFs require dated holdings and expenses. Business inputs require cash-flow timing and working-capital references. Complete input returns `ready_for_offline_evaluation` with no model result and no qualified freshness/authenticity. Relationship attribution links each edge to a separate declared assumption and publisher group, leaving independent review pending. The existing one-hop model now rejects cycles. Structural linkage alone is never eligible for protected scoring.

`backend/financial-monitor-policy.mjs` evaluates offline candidates using explicit consent, cancellation, expiry, matching subject/metric/unit/source, increasing observation dates, fresh retrieval and UTC observation age. Frequency is bounded to at least one hour, receipts to thirty days or rule expiry, and duplicates use semantic values/periods rather than retrieval timestamps or changing transport hashes. Scope is part of the key. The function has no scheduler, persistence, delivery transport or authentication authority. A future authenticated worker must bind the scope server-side, enforce retention, atomically claim idempotency and reconcile uncertain delivery outcomes. Two workers calling a pure function are not durable duplicate suppression. Accessible subscription/cancellation controls and transport-confirmed delivery remain implementation work. Every candidate says `notification_sent:false` and has no delivery confirmation.

## Protected evidence-strength evaluation proposal

Keep the existing 75-point admission law and `evidence_strength:null`. Do not turn source transport success, reference counts, model self-confidence, publisher aliases or fixture passes into evidence support. No scoring formula or calibrated probability is supplied here.

1. Establish an independently adjudicated, dated corpus across bonds, equities/ETFs, macro and business claims, with raw artifacts, publisher lineage and exact subject/period/currency/unit. Keep factual support, economic forecast outcomes and source accuracy as separate labels. Protect private material and use only authorized public or synthetic fixtures initially.
2. Include adversarial false issuer/CIK, instrument substitution, wrong currency/scale, look-ahead publication dates, backdated retrieval, altered hash, stale series, preliminary/amended filings, same-vendor syndication, duplicate IDs, missing periods, negative evidence and genuine contradictions. The missing BLS month observed here is a regression case; it does not establish historical accuracy of other months.
3. Split by publisher lineage, instrument and time to prevent leakage. Freeze a held-out set before fitting any methodology. Have independent reviewers adjudicate disagreements and retain unknown labels. Measure false-supported conclusions, false rejections, abstentions, contradiction detection and results by domain/freshness group, with uncertainty intervals and dataset sizes.
4. Preregister acceptance criteria with the protected-engine owner before evaluation. Require no authority/privacy/identity failures on the adversarial boundary suite, and explicit review of every falsely supported held-out claim. Numerical performance thresholds need owner/methodology acceptance; this candidate does not invent them or claim to satisfy them. A support score must not be labeled an outcome probability.
5. Version the corpus, adjudication, source policies, methodology, calibration and test receipts. Run frozen shadow evaluations with no customer action or charges. Activate scoring only through separate protected-engine qualification and an authorized acceptance gate; retain an abstaining rollback version.

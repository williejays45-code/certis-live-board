# CERTIS Website Blueprint — Locked

## 1. Face Page
The CERTIS wordmark dominates the page. It is animated and contains the visual language of digital products, Bitcoin, XRP, data, and market signals. A continuous digital stream crosses all letters.

## 2. Transition
"Enter CERTIS" causes the wordmark to energize, then moves directly into the Live Board. The stream persists visually so the brand and product feel like one system.

## 3. Live Board
Primary board lanes:
- What changed
- Markets
- Digital products
- Evidence
- Authority
- Guided Mode

## 4. Visual Law
CERTIS should always look alive. Movement should indicate information flow, never random decoration.

## 5. Product Law
Public product. Protected core. Customer access and founder/admin access are distinct trust boundaries.
